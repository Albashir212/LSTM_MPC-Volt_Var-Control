# LSTM-MPC-VAR-BESS-Coordination
This repository contains the complete research pipeline for a forecast-driven, loss-aware Model Predictive Control (MPC) framework for coordinated photovoltaic (PV) inverter reactive power and Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) active power dispatch in unbalanced low-voltage distribution networks, developed by Albashir Bashir at the University of Wollongong in Dubai under the supervision of Dr. Mohamad Nassereddine. The framework integrates causal LSTM-based solar forecasting, nonlinear AC-informed convex quadratic optimization using CVXPY–OSQP, and forecast-conditioned battery state-of-charge scheduling, supported by a nonlinear four-wire AC supervisory layer for dispatch verification and corrective control. The methodology is evaluated on a synthetic Nigerian-informed 28-bus distribution feeder through 8,760-hour annual simulations, controller ablation studies, PV–BESS sizing and robustness analyses, and independent numerical cross-validation using OpenDSS. The loss-aware controller achieves a 20.72% reduction in modeled annual feeder losses relative to the original loss-unaware MPC while reducing primary-band overvoltage hours from 84 to zero, with battery utilization and conversion-loss trade-offs explicitly investigated. The research builds upon an IEEE ICECCME 2026 conference paper and is being extended through comparative benchmarking and additional validation toward submission to IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid.
